\section{Summary}

Ph.D.\ in chemistry with over 10 years of experience in mass spectrometry-based proteomics. My doctoral
work culminated in three first-author publications (\textit{JACS}, \textit{Anal.\ Chem.}, \textit{JASMS}),
two NIH grants (F31, T32), and spanned a broad range of wet lab techniques including cell culture, protein
purification, and recombinant DNA methods --- giving me a strong and versatile foundation at the bench.
Since joining Pacific Northwest National Laboratory as a postdoctoral researcher, my focus has shifted
toward statistical analysis and interpretation of large-scale proteomics datasets. I have thrived in a
fast-paced, highly collaborative environment where I contribute to many diverse projects simultaneously,
and this breadth of work has sharpened my ability to communicate complex scientific concepts to both
specialist and non-specialist audiences. Overall, I have extensive experience with all facets of proteomics
including native and denatured top-down, single-cell proteomics, traditional bottom-up, and selected
reaction monitoring.
